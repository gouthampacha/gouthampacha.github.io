I'm a proud [Clemson University](https://www.clemson.edu/) graduate. Once a Tiger, always a Tiger. My time at Clemson shaped my engineering mindset and instilled a drive to solve hard problems with creative, practical approaches.

I'm a hobby teacher: I find joy in breaking down complex topics and making them accessible. Whether it's mentoring junior engineers, writing documentation, or explaining distributed consensus to someone over coffee, teaching is a core part of who I am.

I'm also an animal lover. If you have a dog, we're probably already friends.
